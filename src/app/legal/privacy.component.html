In short

Loop Workouts has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no cookies. Your workouts, exercises, history and settings are saved in this browser, on this device, and are never sent to us. We can't see them.

What's saved on your device

  • Your workouts and your own exercises.
  • Your history: when you worked out, for how long, the sets, reps and weights you logged, and any notes you added.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, kg or lb, and whether sound, vibration and keeping the screen on are on.
  • The workout in progress, so a refresh carries on where you were.

They're kept in your browser's storage for this site. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them, and so can the browser itself if the device runs short of space. To keep a copy, use Export backup in Settings: the file goes wherever you save it, and we never get a copy.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the app. Loop Workouts is hosted by Vercel. As with any website, Vercel's servers see your IP address, your browser's details and the pages it asks for, and may keep them in logs for a while to run and protect the service. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Fonts and icons. These are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address and browser's details when they're fetched. See Google's privacy policy.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. Your workouts and history never leave the device.

Copy AI prompt

Copy AI prompt in Settings only copies some text to your clipboard. If you paste it into an AI chat, with a plan of your own, that service's privacy policy covers what you share there.

Your choices

As we hold none of your data, there's nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. You can do all of that yourself: see and change everything in the app, export it all with Export backup, and delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If Loop Workouts ever saves your data anywhere else, such as syncing it between your devices with an account, this notice will say so before it happens, and the date at the top will change.
