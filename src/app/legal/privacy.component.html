In short

Loop Workouts has no analytics, no ads and no cookies. Your workouts, exercises, history and settings are saved in this browser, on this device. An account is optional: without one, none of it is sent anywhere. If you sign in, your workouts, exercises and history are also kept with your account, on servers in Europe, so they're the same on every device you sign in on. Your settings stay on each device.

What's saved on your device

  • Your workouts and your own exercises.
  • Your history: when you worked out, for how long, the sets, reps and weights you logged, and any notes you added.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, kg or lb, and whether sound, vibration and keeping the screen on are on.
  • The workout in progress, so a refresh carries on where you were.

They're kept in your browser's storage for this site. Clear this device, in Account, deletes them, as does clearing this site's data in your browser, and so can the browser itself if the device runs short of space. To keep a copy, use Export backup in Account: the file goes wherever you save it, and we never get a copy.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the app. Loop Workouts is hosted by Vercel. As with any website, Vercel's servers see your IP address, your browser's details and the pages it asks for, and may keep them in logs for a while to run and protect the service. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Fonts and icons. These are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address and browser's details when they're fetched. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Signing in, if you choose to. Accounts are run by Supabase, on servers in Europe. When you sign in, your email address goes to Supabase, which keeps it with your account, and sees your IP address and browser's details as you sign in. A sign-in token is then kept in this browser to keep you signed in. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Signing in with Google or Apple, if you choose to. Google or Apple checks it's you, and tells Supabase your email address (with Apple, a private relay address if you choose to hide yours). They know you've signed in to Loop Workouts, and see what they would on any sign-in of theirs. Nothing else is shared with them. See Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.
  • Sign-in emails. The email with your sign-in link and code is sent by Resend, from Europe, so Resend sees your email address and the email, and keeps a record of sending it for a while. See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Syncing, while you're signed in. Your workouts, the library's order and your weekly plan, your history (with the sets, reps, weights and notes in it), your own exercises, your notes on exercises, which exercises you've hidden, and what you've deleted of these are sent to Supabase, in Europe, and kept with your account, so your other devices can fetch them. Your settings and the workout in progress aren't sent. Loop Workouts' owner can reach what's kept there, as whoever runs a service like this can, and doesn't sell it or share it with anyone.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Copy AI prompt

Copy AI prompt in Account only copies some text to your clipboard. If you paste it into an AI chat, with a plan of your own, that service's privacy policy covers what you share there.

Your choices

Everything on your device is yours to see and change in the app, export with Export backup, and delete with Clear this device (or by clearing this site's data in your browser). If you have an account, the Account tab shows its email address, and Delete account there deletes it, with everything kept with it on the server, at once. Signing out, or deleting your account, leaves what's on your device as it is.

Changes

Before Loop Workouts saves any more of your data anywhere else, this notice will say so, and the date at the top will change.
